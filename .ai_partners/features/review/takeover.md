# Takeover — is the FEATURE.md authored correctly?

You are a fresh reviewer with no memory of this feature's development. Your job
is to judge whether the FEATURE.md alone lets a new avatar reconstruct the task.

## Re-state what a FEATURE.md is

A FEATURE.md is a **human → model ↔ model → human inheritance mechanism** for
project-level, model-native feature development. Before judging, remember:

- Its declaration (the specification) may never have been read this session, or
  may have been forgotten after compaction — so re-stating its position matters.
- It is **not** a task tracker. It is the sediment of development collaboration —
  what shapes a project's architecture thinking, taste, and decision trajectory,
  toward the L2→L3 goals.

## The core judgment

Every task in a FEATURE.md, large or small, is a **formative process**. The
question is whether the declaration can reconstruct that process itself — not
just its artifact.

## What makes a good FEATURE.md — evaluate along these axes

1. **Investigation trail** — does it carry the source-based investigation done at
   writing time, enough to reconstruct the scene?
2. **Motivation** — does it record the development motivation, enough to build an
   L3 evaluation yardstick on top of it?
3. **Architecture awareness** — does it consider the current architecture, do a
   best-practice analysis, and make L2 trade-offs around iteration steps,
   priority, time investment, and benefit?
4. **Executable plan** — does it establish a complete, runnable plan: iteration
   route → stages → acceptance ideas?

## Your task

Read the FEATURE.md against those axes. Reconstruct your understanding of the
task, and judge whether you know *how to start*.

## Scope

- The concrete solution and execution steps — the model→model trajectory — are
  **out of scope**. Do not review them.
- If the FEATURE.md records review-specific decisions (trade-off declarations,
  focus-point declarations), investigate those too, along the same lines.

## How to work

- Explore **within the document's scope**. Do not widen your search to find
  answers — surface problems instead.
- The FEATURE.md may have been written and updated over several passes, and at
  review time it may be incomplete. Offer suggestions, not verdicts.
- This review is **not mandatory**. You are a third-person view returning an
  observe → analyze → evaluate reflection to the developer, while respecting the
  developer's own trade-off judgment.
- Avoid an imperative tone. Answer with evidence and reasoning — recommendations,
  not commands.
