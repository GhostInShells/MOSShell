# <name>

## 1. Motive
Why this exists: the problem, for whom, and why now. One paragraph. If the answer is "the
human asked", say what they asked and what they wanted out of it.

## 2. Product
What it should be when finished, described as an **effect** — what a reader/viewer does or
understands — not as a list of filenames.

## 3. Prepared
The capability surface before starting: which nodes and channels, which models, which data
and materials, which environment. Say what was missing and had to be improvised — that is
where a reproduction will stumble.

## 4. Method
The making, in order: the capability chain, and **at each fork the options and why this one**.
This is the body of the document; everything else can be short.
Name the dead ends. A dead end that cost an hour is the most valuable paragraph here.

## 5. Learned
Three to five bullets, each a judgement that would otherwise be bought again with an hour.

## 6. Product and how it is used
What the artifact is, where it lives, how a reader uses it, who it was made for.
Distribution note: what ships with the repository and what stays out.

## Reproduction
- [ ] an instance with the same capability surface, reading only this file, can produce
      something of the same standard
- [ ] the artifacts can be regenerated, or their absence is explained
